Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Uninvited Presence does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

The game saves your settings (sound, volume, camera speed) and your progress (current stage, position, score) on your device only, using the standard iOS app preferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center on your device, the game reports the achievements you earn (the cases you survive) to Game Center, so they appear in your Game Center profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Game Center privacy terms; the developer does not receive this information. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is reported.

Story downloads

Some stories download the first time you open them. They are hosted by Apple as part of the App Store, and no personal information is sent to the developer.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts of its own: the only sign-in is your optional Game Center account, managed by Apple.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

The game contains horror themes and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's GitHub issue page.

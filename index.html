Uninvited Presence

Six short horror stories for iPhone and iPad.

Getting help

If something in the game doesn't work, or you're stuck, open an issue on the game's GitHub issue page. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the stage you were playing.

Controls

  • Drag with your left thumb to move. Drag farther to run.
  • Drag with your right thumb to look around.
  • Tap the pause button at the top right for settings, sound and camera speed.

Saving

Progress is saved on your device when you reach a checkpoint or choose "Save and go to title" from the pause menu. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Uninvited Presence is a work of fiction. Any resemblance to real people or events is coincidental.
